Why use this site

Built for fast, private estimates

Accurate formulas

Core loan, investment, GST, and percentage formulas are separated from UI rendering and include self-checks for common examples.

No backend

Your inputs stay in the browser. The project can be hosted as static files without accounts, databases, or paid APIs.

Useful outputs

Result cards, explanations, copy buttons, reset buttons, and CSV downloads help you move from estimate to spreadsheet quickly.

Instant results

All calculations happen in real-time, entirely within your browser. No waiting, no server round-trips, no delays.

Multi-currency

The currency converter lets you enter your own exchange rates so results always reflect the rates you actually use.

Export data

Download amortization tables, SIP growth tables, and salary breakdowns as CSV files for use in spreadsheets.
